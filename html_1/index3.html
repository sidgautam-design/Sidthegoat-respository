Scenario 01 — Phishing Detection
Suspicious Email Received

You just got this email. Is it safe, or is it a phishing attack? Analyze carefully!

FROM: security-alerts@paypa1-support.net
TO: you@youremail.com
TIME: Today, 2:47 AM
⚠️ URGENT: Your account has been SUSPENDED!
Dear Valued Customer,

We have detected suspicious activity on your PayPal account. Your account will be permanently deleted in 24 hours unless you verify your identity immediately.

Click here to restore your account now:
→ http://paypa1-verify-login.xyz/restore?token=abc123

Regards,
PayPal Security Team™

⚠ RED FLAGS SPOTTED

Sender domain is "paypa1-support.net" (fake! uses number 1 not letter l)
Sent at 2:47 AM — bots don't sleep
Creates panic with "24 hours" urgency
Link goes to "paypa1-verify-login.xyz" — NOT paypal.com
No personal name used — just "Valued Customer"
